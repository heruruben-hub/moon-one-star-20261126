Entry for [The Moon Gets One Star](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-moon-gets-one-star-768236e5) on make.fun: the funniest one-star review of the Moon, 120 characters at most.

## The review

> One star. Never on time, vanishes for a week every month, and still takes full credit for the tides. HR does nothing.

117 characters. The angle is not the stock crater, cheese or brightness joke: the Moon is reviewed like a bad coworker — it is never on time, it disappears for a week every month (the new moon), and it still takes full credit for the tides, while HR does nothing about it. A grievance only the Moon could earn, in the voice of a genuine one-star reviewer.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the review, exactly as written, to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, no trailing newline), after validating it against the competition gate: 1–120 characters, not only white space, single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
